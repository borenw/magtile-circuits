🕰️ Master of Time — build the oscillator

Fill the greyed-out 3×3 schematic with the right tiles. Close the loop and the RC oscillator drives a piezo — it sings.

← build guide🔦 Make it bright🎚️ Stay grounded
The mission: each grey square shows a faint component or wire — match it with a tile from the tray. A straight/elbow wire, a resistor R1, a capacitor C1, the inverter, and a piezo buzzer. Closed, the inverter keeps charging and discharging C1 through R1 — a relaxation oscillator. With C1 ≈ 1 nF, R1 ≈ 470 kΩ it runs near 2.7 kHz, right in the piezo's loud band. Tap a tile then a square, or drag it over.
