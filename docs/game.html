🕰️ Master of Time — build the oscillator

Fill the greyed-out 3×3 schematic with the right tiles. Get all nine correct and the RC oscillator comes alive.

The mission: each grey square shows a faint component or wire. Find the matching tile in the tray and drop it in — a straight wire, an elbow, a resistor, a capacitor, the inverter, and a buzzer. When the whole loop is closed, the inverter charges and discharges the capacitor through the resistor over and over — a relaxation oscillator — and the buzzer sings.
