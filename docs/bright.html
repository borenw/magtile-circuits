🔦 Make it Bright — light the LED

The simplest circuit there is: a battery, an LED, and two wires. Close the loop on the 2×2 grid and it glows.

← build guide🕰️ Master of Time🎚️ Stay grounded
The mission: current has to leave the battery’s ＋ terminal, pass through the LED, and return to the － terminal — one closed loop. Drop the battery and LED into their squares, then the two elbow wires that finish the ring. No loop, no light.
