🎚️ Stay Grounded — the touch theremin

Same oscillator, but the RC node is a touch pad and there’s a ground pad. Your body becomes part of the circuit.

← build guide🕰️ Master of Time🔦 Make it bright
The mission: build the piezo oscillator, but this time R1 is huge (~1 MΩ) and C1 tiny (~1 nF) so the input node is high-impedance — sensitive to your finger. Touch the ✋ node and it adds a little body capacitance; touch it while your other hand references the ground pad and the pitch bends. That’s why engineers say stay grounded: signals only make sense against a common ground.
